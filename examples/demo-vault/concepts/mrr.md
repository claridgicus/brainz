**Monthly Recurring Revenue**: the normalized monthly value of all active
subscriptions. Acme CRM reports MRR per team and attributes changes to
campaigns and reps.

New MRR is attributed to the converting [[lead]] and, where applicable, the
last-touch campaign from [[email-campaigns]].
