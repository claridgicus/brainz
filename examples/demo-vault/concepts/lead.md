A **lead** is a [[contact-model|contact]] that has shown buying intent but is
not yet an opportunity — e.g. signed up for a trial, replied to a campaign, or
requested a demo.

Leads are ranked by [[lead-scoring]]; a score ≥ 70 marks a lead as *qualified*
and routes it to a rep.
